Our Services

Complete Diagnostic Care Under One Roof

From imaging to lab testing, we offer the essential diagnostic services your family needs — with modern equipment and a caring team.

OPD & Consultation

ओपीडी तथा परामर्श

General OPD, Consultant OPD, Gynae OPD, ENT OPD and Ortho OPD — expert consultation for the whole family.

Pharmacy

फार्मेसी

In-house pharmacy stocked with essential and prescribed medicines, available on-site.

Laboratory (Lab Tests)

प्रयोगशाला (ल्याब जाँच)

Blood, urine, stool, H. Pylori, allergy test, thyroid, arthritis-related tests, ANC, serology and more.

Imaging & Diagnostics

इमेजिङ तथा डायग्नोस्टिक्स

Digital X-Ray, Ultrasound and ECG for fast, accurate diagnosis.

Emergency & Critical Care

आपतकालीन तथा गम्भीर स्याहार

Emergency care with patient monitor and oxygen, plus daycare and nebulizer services.

Surgical & Minor Procedures

शल्यक्रिया तथा साना प्रक्रिया

Minor OT, general surgical procedures, suturing and plastering for fractures.

Eye & Dental Care

आँखा तथा दाँत स्याहार

General eye and dental checkups, plus an in-house glasses (चश्मा घर) counter.

Chronic & Specialized Care

दीर्घरोग तथा विशेष स्याहार

Sugar, blood pressure, thyroid and heart disease treatment, skin treatment, and infertility treatment & advice.

Everyday Essentials

दैनिक उपभोग्य सामग्री

Select food and cosmetic products also available at the center.
